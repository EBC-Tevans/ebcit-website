Managed IT Services

Let EBCIT be your dedicated IT department. We provide comprehensive, proactive management of your entire technology environment so your team can focus on what matters most — running your business.

  • Help desk and end-user support (Tier 1–3)
  • Patch management and software updates
  • Network administration and optimization
  • Server, workstation, and device management

Why It Matters

Downtime is expensive. Reactive IT support means your organization loses productivity waiting for problems to be fixed. Our managed services model shifts that equation — we identify and resolve issues before they become outages, giving you a predictable monthly cost and peace of mind.

EBCIT's team works as a seamless extension of your organization, understanding your mission and building an IT roadmap aligned with your short- and long-term goals.

Cybersecurity Solutions

In an era of sophisticated threats, your organization's data and systems are constantly at risk. EBCIT delivers layered, proactive cybersecurity programs that protect what matters — from endpoints to the cloud.

What We Offer

  • Security risk assessments and gap analysis
  • Vulnerability scanning and penetration testing
  • Security Information and Event Management (SIEM)
  • Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR)
  • Identity and Access Management (IAM / Zero Trust)
  • Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) implementation
  • Incident response planning and tabletop exercises
  • Compliance readiness (NIST, CMMC, FedRAMP, HIPAA, SOC 2)
  • Security awareness training for staff

Our Approach

We begin with a thorough assessment of your current security posture — mapping your assets, identifying weaknesses, and benchmarking against industry frameworks such as NIST SP 800-53 and the CMMC model. From there, we develop a prioritized remediation roadmap and implement controls that fit your budget and risk tolerance.

Our team stays current with evolving threat intelligence, ensuring your defenses adapt as attackers do.

Compliance & Government Focus

For government contractors and agencies, we specialize in navigating complex compliance requirements — including FedRAMP authorization support, FISMA reporting, and CMMC certification preparation — so you can pursue and maintain contracts with confidence.
