Security Auditing & Advisory

Effective security starts with an objective understanding of risk. EBCIT assesses your environment, tests the design and operation of key controls, and gives leadership a clear view of what needs attention and why.

What We Offer

  • Enterprise security posture and risk assessments
  • Security control design and effectiveness reviews
  • Vulnerability assessment and testing coordination
  • Identity, access, and privileged account reviews
  • Security policy and governance evaluations
  • Incident response plan reviews and tabletop exercises
  • Third-party and supply chain security assessments
  • Remediation validation and finding closure reviews
  • AI-assisted log analysis and threat-pattern detection to surface findings across large environments
  • Remediation planning and validation using industry-standard and community-backed open-source security tools

Evidence-Based Assessments

We combine stakeholder interviews, documentation review, technical evidence, and control testing to distinguish material risk from noise. Findings are tied to business impact and presented in language that technical teams and decision-makers can act on.

From Findings to Remediation

Each engagement produces a prioritized roadmap with practical recommendations, responsible owners, and measurable next steps. When additional support is needed, we can advise on or assist with remediation while preserving a clear focus on independent assessment and validation. Where appropriate we validate fixes against the original findings, so remediation moves from a promise on paper to a checked, repeatable result.

Where scale makes manual review impractical, we apply AI-driven analysis to aggregate log data, correlate events, and highlight control gaps that traditional sampling might miss. The technology accelerates evidence gathering; professional judgment drives every conclusion.

Compliance Consulting

Compliance requires more than a checklist. EBCIT helps organizations interpret requirements, evaluate current controls, organize evidence, and build sustainable programs that stand up to review.

What We Offer

  • Readiness assessments and control gap analysis
  • NIST, CMMC, FedRAMP, FISMA, HIPAA, and SOC 2 advisory
  • Control mapping and responsibility matrices
  • Policy, standard, and procedure development
  • Evidence collection and documentation review
  • Plan of Action and Milestones development
  • Audit and assessment preparation support
  • Ongoing compliance program and governance advisory
  • AI-powered evidence review and continuous compliance monitoring

Practical Compliance

We translate requirements into controls that fit your operating environment. The result is a defensible compliance roadmap that identifies gaps, required evidence, priorities, dependencies, and accountable owners.

Government and Regulated Environments

Our consulting supports agencies, government contractors, nonprofits, and commercial organizations facing demanding contractual or regulatory obligations. We help teams prepare for external review and establish repeatable processes that reduce future audit burden.

We use AI tools to accelerate document review, flag policy drift, and identify control weaknesses across large evidence sets. Continuous monitoring surfaces issues between formal assessments, giving teams earlier visibility and reducing last-minute remediation pressure.

Financial Forensics & Audit Advisory

When financial activity raises questions, EBCIT helps organizations establish the facts. We analyze records, trace transactions, evaluate controls, and communicate findings with the clarity needed for informed action.

What We Offer

  • Financial anomaly and fraud-risk assessments
  • Transaction tracing and account reconciliation analysis
  • Procurement, expense, and vendor payment reviews
  • Revenue, cash receipt, and disbursement reviews
  • Internal control evaluation and testing
  • Audit readiness and supporting schedule preparation
  • Financial data analysis and exception testing
  • Findings documentation and corrective action advisory
  • AI-driven pattern recognition and anomaly detection across high-volume transaction data

Methodical and Defensible

Our work follows documented procedures, preserves source evidence, and separates verified facts from assumptions. Analyses are reproducible, and reports connect each conclusion to the records and control conditions that support it.

When We Help

Engagements may address unexplained variances, suspected misuse of funds, grant or contract spending concerns, audit findings, control failures, or financial due diligence. We tailor the scope to the question at hand and provide actionable recommendations without overstating the evidence.

For engagements involving large transaction populations, we use AI-assisted analysis to help identify statistical outliers, duplicate patterns, and unusual timing clusters that warrant closer examination.
